**History** lists every send, newest first, grouped by day. That includes scheduled messages, messages sent with **Send now**, and anything an [agent](/agents/overview/) sent.

Each row shows the time, who it went to, the text **as it went out** with its variables filled in, and a status. A send an agent caused is marked with the agent's name.

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Sending** | Queued and waiting its turn, or going out now. Each account sends one message every 3 seconds at most. |
| **Waiting to retry** | A try failed in a way that's worth repeating. The reason is shown, and the next try follows by itself. |
| **Sent** | WhatsApp accepted the message. |
| **Failed** | It didn't go out, or Whatomato can't tell. The reason is shown. |

:::note
**Sent** means WhatsApp acknowledged the message. It doesn't prove the message reached the phone, or that anyone read it.
:::

## Why a send fails

| Reason shown | What happened |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp wasn't connected | The connection was down. Whatomato retries for about half an hour. |
| WhatsApp couldn't take this message | WhatsApp refused it for now. Retried the same way. |
| WhatsApp isn't linked | No WhatsApp account is linked. [Link one](/guides/link-whatsapp/). |
| WhatsApp is paused | You paused the connection. Reconnect it on the WhatsApp page. |
| WhatsApp has stopped; reconnect it on the WhatsApp page | A takeover, a ban or a refusal stopped the connection. |
| This number isn't on WhatsApp | The number has no WhatsApp account. |
| WhatsApp rejected this message | WhatsApp returned an error, shown with its code. |
| WhatsApp didn't confirm this message, so it may or may not have gone out | No acknowledgement came within 20 seconds. Check the chat on your phone before sending again. |
| The server restarted during this send, so it may or may not have gone out | The same uncertainty, caused by a restart. |
| Not sent: … | A scheduled message that belongs to an agent wasn't allowed to run. The text says why, for instance that the connection is paused or over a limit. |

Only the first two are retried. The rest need you, and the two "may or may not have gone out" reasons are never retried because a retry could deliver a second copy.

## Finding things

History shows 25 sends a page and has no search. To see what went to one person, open them under **Contacts**: their page lists what was sent recently.

History isn't cleared. Deleting a scheduled message keeps the record of what it already sent.
