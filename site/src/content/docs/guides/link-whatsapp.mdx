Whatomato joins your account the way WhatsApp Web does, as a linked device. Your phone keeps working as usual, and messages go out from your own number.

Each Whatomato account links one WhatsApp account. Linking and unlinking ask for your Whatomato password again, or a [passkey](/guides/account-security/#passkeys) if you've added one.

:::caution
WhatsApp doesn't officially allow apps like Whatomato and can ban a number that uses one. See [WhatsApp's rules](/start/whatsapp-risk/).
:::

## Link with a QR code

You need the phone that has WhatsApp, and a second screen to show the code on.

<Screenshot name="whatsapp-not-linked" alt="The WhatsApp page before linking, with the buttons Link with QR code and Link with phone number instead" />

<Steps>

1. In Whatomato, open **WhatsApp** and choose **Link with QR code**.
2. Enter your password and choose **Continue**, or choose **Use a passkey**.

   <Screenshot name="whatsapp-link-password" alt="The Link with a QR code dialog asking for the account password" />
3. On your phone, open WhatsApp. Tap **Settings** on iPhone, or **⋮** on Android.
4. Tap **Linked devices**, then **Link a device**.
5. Point your phone at the code on the screen.

   <Screenshot name="whatsapp-qr" alt="The WhatsApp page showing a QR code to scan, the four steps to follow on the phone, and when the code expires" />

</Steps>

## Link with a phone number

Use this when Whatomato is open on the same phone that has WhatsApp, so there's nothing to point the camera at.

<Steps>

1. In Whatomato, open **WhatsApp** and choose **Link with phone number instead**.
2. Enter the number of the phone that has WhatsApp, with its country code, then your password, and choose **Continue**. With a passkey, choose **Use a passkey** instead of entering the password.
3. Whatomato shows an 8-character code. **Copy code** copies it.

   <Screenshot name="whatsapp-pair-code" alt="The WhatsApp page showing an 8-character pairing code, a Copy code button and the steps to follow on the phone" />
4. On your phone, open WhatsApp. Tap **Settings** on iPhone, or **⋮** on Android.
5. Tap **Linked devices**, then **Link a device**.
6. Tap **Link with phone number instead** and enter the code.

</Steps>

## While you're linking

- Each code expires quickly, and a new one replaces it by itself. The page counts down to each change.
- A linking attempt ends after 5 minutes. QR codes run out sooner, after about 3 minutes. If that happens you see **Linking stopped**; start again for a new code.
- Linking runs on the server, so leaving the page doesn't stop it. **Cancel** does.
- You can start linking 5 times, then once every 10 minutes.

If the page says there's **no room for another WhatsApp account on this server**, the instance has reached the number of accounts its operator allows. Try again later.

## After your phone links

The page shows **WhatsApp is linked** while it syncs for the first time, which can take a few minutes. You can leave the page while it does. Whatomato emails you to say a number was linked, and your phone starts sharing its saved [contacts](/guides/recipients/).

Once it says **WhatsApp is connected**, **Send a test message** sends a short message to your own "Message yourself" chat, to prove that sending works.

<Screenshot name="whatsapp-connected" alt="The WhatsApp page once linked: the number, WhatsApp is connected, Pause and Unlink buttons, a Send a test message button and the list of groups" />

On your phone, Whatomato is listed under **Linked devices** with your other devices.

## What each status means

The **WhatsApp** item in the side navigation carries a status dot, and the page says more.

| The page says | What it means | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **WhatsApp is linked** | The first sync is running. | Wait, or **Unlink**. |
| **WhatsApp is connected** | Messages go out from your number. | **Pause**, **Unlink**. |
| **Reconnecting to WhatsApp** | The connection dropped and Whatomato is reconnecting by itself. Sends wait, and keep trying for about half an hour. | Nothing is needed. |
| **WhatsApp is paused** | You disconnected it and kept the link. Nothing is sent. | **Reconnect**, **Unlink**. |
| **Another session took over** | Something else connected with the keys that link Whatomato to your account. | **Unlink**, then link again for new keys. |
| **WhatsApp has banned this number for now** | A temporary ban. The page gives its end time when WhatsApp does. | **Reconnect** once the ban is over. |
| **Whatomato needs an update** | WhatsApp stopped accepting the version Whatomato connects with. | Try **Reconnect** later. |
| **WhatsApp refused the connection** | WhatsApp gave no reason. | **Reconnect**. If it keeps happening, unlink and link again. |

<Screenshot name="whatsapp-stopped" alt="The WhatsApp page after another session took over: a red status line, an Unlink button and a Reconnect link" />

You get an email when a number is linked or unlinked, when the device is removed from your phone, when another session takes over, and when WhatsApp bans the number. Pausing and reconnecting send no email.

## Pause and reconnect

**Pause** disconnects Whatomato and keeps the link, so reconnecting needs no new code. Neither asks for your password.

<Screenshot name="whatsapp-paused" alt="The WhatsApp page while paused, with Reconnect and Unlink buttons" />

While it's paused, nothing is sent. A scheduled message that comes due **fails, and doesn't go out later**, so pause only when that is what you want.

<Screenshot name="whatsapp-paused-messages" alt="The Messages page while WhatsApp is paused, with a notice that scheduled messages won't send and a Reconnect button" />

WhatsApp logs out a linked device that stays offline too long. To keep the link alive, a paused account connects for about two minutes once a week and sends nothing.

## Unlink

**Unlink** asks for your password or a passkey. Whatomato then logs out of your WhatsApp and deletes the keys that linked it, together with its copy of your contacts and the messages it kept for agents to read. Your scheduled messages, templates and history stay; the messages stop sending until you link again.

If WhatsApp can't be reached, the keys are deleted anyway and the page tells you to **remove Whatomato from your phone too**: open **Linked devices** on your phone, tap Whatomato, then **Log out**.

Removing Whatomato under **Linked devices** on your phone does the same as unlinking here.

## Staying linked

- WhatsApp logs out every linked device when the phone itself stays unused for about two weeks. Nothing in Whatomato can prevent that, so open WhatsApp on your phone now and then.
- A connected account shows as online for 45 seconds once a day, which keeps the phone from flagging the device as inactive.
- An account stopped by a takeover or a ban stays offline until you reconnect it.
