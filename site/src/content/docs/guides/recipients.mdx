## Contacts

Your phone shares its address book with every linked device, and Whatomato keeps a copy so a message can go to a name. The first connection after linking brings the whole book over once. After that each change on the phone arrives as it's made.

The **Contacts** page lists everyone, grouped A to Z. Search matches any part of a name, or two or more digits of a number.

<Screenshot name="contacts-search" alt="The Contacts page with a search for an, listing the four contacts whose names contain it" />

Choose someone to see what's scheduled to them and what went out recently, and to write to them with **New message** or **Send now**.

<Screenshot name="contact-detail" alt="A contact's panel: New message and Send now buttons, the message scheduled to them, and what was sent to them recently" />

A few things to know:

- **Contacts can't be added or edited in Whatomato.** Change them on your phone and the change comes through while WhatsApp is linked.
- **Number not shared** means WhatsApp hasn't given linked devices that person's number. Whatomato can't send to them.
- A contact with no name saved is left out.
- The copy stays readable while WhatsApp is paused. A paused account doesn't receive changes.
- [Unlinking](/guides/link-whatsapp/#unlink) deletes the copy.

Names are stored encrypted. See [What's stored](/reference/data/).

## A number that isn't saved

In a message's **Send to** box, type the whole number with its country code, for example `+1 212 555 0123`. The number may hold digits, spaces, `+`, `-` and brackets, and has 8 to 15 digits. It can't start with 0: write the country code in place of the leading zero.

Whatomato offers the number at the top of the list, marked **Not in your contacts**. It offers only a number that is valid for its country code; for anything else the list says **No contact matches**.

<Screenshot name="recipient-number" alt="A number typed into the contact field, offered in the list as Not in your contacts" />

Whether the number is on WhatsApp is checked when the message is due, not when you save. A number that isn't shows in [History](/guides/history/) as failed with "This number isn't on WhatsApp".

:::caution
Messaging people who don't have your number saved is one of the things that gets numbers banned. See [WhatsApp's rules](/start/whatsapp-risk/).
:::

## Groups

Whatomato can send to any group your number is in. Groups aren't stored: they're fetched from WhatsApp when needed, so they only load while WhatsApp is connected.

- In a message, choose **Group** under **Send to** and pick one.
- The **WhatsApp** page lists the groups your number is in while it's connected.
- Groups don't appear on the Contacts page.

A message keeps the group's name as it was when you picked it. A group with no name shows as **Unnamed group**.

<Screenshot name="recipient-group" alt="The Send to field set to Group, with the list of groups open" />

## Profile pictures

Pictures of saved contacts and groups show while WhatsApp is connected. They're looked up when the app needs them and kept in the server's memory for a few hours, never in the database. At other times you see initials.
