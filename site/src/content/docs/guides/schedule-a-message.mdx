Open **Messages** and choose **New message**. The form asks who the message goes to, what it says and when it goes out. Nothing is checked until you choose **Schedule message**.

<Screenshot name="message-form" alt="The New message form: a contact, the message with a first-name variable and how it will read, a one-time date and time, and the Enabled switch" />

## Who it goes to

Under **Send to**, choose **Contact** or **Group**.

<Screenshot name="recipient-picker" alt="The contact field with an typed in and the matching contacts listed under it" />

- **Contact** searches the contacts saved on your phone by name or number. To send to someone who isn't saved, type their whole number with its country code.
- **Group** lists the groups your number is in. It needs WhatsApp to be connected.

Whatomato doesn't check that a number is on WhatsApp when you save. It checks when the message is due, and a number that isn't on WhatsApp shows in History as failed. [Contacts and groups](/guides/recipients/) has the details.

## What it says

Write up to 4096 characters under **Message**.

If you have saved [templates](/guides/templates/), **Apply template** replaces the text with a template's. Type `{{` to add a variable such as `{{first_name}}` or `{{date}}`, which is filled in as the message goes out. Once the text holds a variable, the form shows it filled in under **Goes out as**, with the real recipient and send time.

## When it goes out

Under **When**, choose **One time** or **Recurring**.

### One time

Pick a date and time under **Send at**. It has to be in the future.

### Recurring

Choose how it repeats under **Repeats**:

| Option | You choose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Days of the week** | Any of Mon to Sun | Monday to Thursday at 9:00 AM |
| **Day of the month** | A day from 1 to 31, or **Last day** | 15th of every month at 9:00 AM |
| **Weekday of the month** | First, Second, Third, Fourth or Last, and a weekday | First Monday of every month at 9:00 AM |
| **Date each year** | A month and a day | Every year on 12 March at 9:00 AM |
| **Custom cron** | A five-field cron expression | `0 9 * * 1-5` |

There's no separate daily option: **Days of the week** with all seven days on is every day, and that's what a new recurring message starts with.

A day of the month that some months don't have is skipped in those months. For the end of every month, choose **Last day**. A message set for 29 February goes out in leap years only.

Then choose the time under **At**:

- **A set time**: a time of day, to the minute.
- **Sunrise** or **Sunset**: the moment itself, or from 5 minutes to 2 hours before or after it.

Below the form, Whatomato writes the schedule out as a sentence and lists the next three times it will send. [Schedules](/reference/schedules/) covers cron syntax and how sunrise and sunset are worked out.

<Screenshot name="message-recurring" alt="The form set to repeat on Mondays, Wednesdays and Fridays at 6:00 PM, with the schedule written out and its next three times listed" />

### Sunrise and sunset

Sun times depend on where you are. Whatomato starts from the main city of your time zone and says so: "Times for Dhaka, the main city of the time zone." **Use my location** asks your browser where you are and uses that instead, rounded to about 10 km.

<Screenshot name="message-sun" alt="The form set to every day, 45 minutes before sunset, with the city the times are for and a Use my location button" />

On a day with no sunrise or sunset, as happens near the poles, the message skips to the next day that has one.

### Time zones

A message keeps the time zone of the browser it was created in. A recurring message set for 9:00 goes out at 9:00 in that zone, through daylight-saving changes too. Editing or duplicating a message keeps its zone, and the zone can't be changed afterwards. To move a message to another zone, create it again from a browser set to that zone.

## Keep it without sending it

The **Enabled** switch at the bottom of the form, and the switch on each row of the Messages page, turn a message off without deleting it.

<Screenshot name="messages-list" alt="The Messages page: seven scheduled messages, each with who it goes to, how it repeats, its next send and a switch" />

Switching a recurring message back on continues from its next time; the runs it missed while off aren't sent. A one-time message whose time has passed can't be switched on: edit it and pick a new time.

## Send something right away

**Send now** on the Messages page sends a message without scheduling it. It asks only who and what. The message joins the same queue as scheduled ones, shows in [History](/guides/history/), and leaves nothing on the Messages page.

<Screenshot name="send-now" alt="The Send a message now dialog, which asks only for a recipient and the text" />

Send now is refused while WhatsApp isn't linked, is paused or has stopped.

## Change or remove a message

Each row's **⋯** menu has:

<Screenshot name="messages-row-menu" alt="A message's menu open on the Messages page, with Edit, Duplicate and Delete" />

- **Edit**, which replaces any part of the message. The next send is worked out again from the moment you save.
- **Duplicate**, which opens a copy to change and save as a new message.
- **Delete**. What the message already sent stays in History.

Once some messages are off or have run, the page splits into three tabs: **Scheduled**, **Off** and **Past**. A one-time message moves to **Past** after it runs, where its menu offers **Schedule again**.

<Screenshot name="messages-past" alt="The Past tab, listing a one-time message and when it ran" />

:::note
**Schedule again** opens the message with its old time, which has passed. Pick a new one before you save.
:::

Switching a message off or deleting it doesn't recall a send that is already on its way or waiting to retry.

A message an [agent](/agents/overview/) scheduled is marked with the agent's name. Editing it makes it yours, and the agent's limits stop applying to it.

## What happens when a message is due

- **Pace.** Each account sends at most one message every 3 seconds, oldest first, however many are due.
- **Reconnecting.** If WhatsApp is reconnecting, the send waits and tries again: after 1, 2, 4, 8 and 15 minutes, six tries over about half an hour. History shows it as **Waiting to retry**.
- **Not linked, paused or stopped.** The send fails straight away and doesn't go out later, because only you can fix that.
- **Unsure whether it went.** If WhatsApp doesn't confirm a message, or the server restarts in the middle of a send, the send fails with a note that it may or may not have gone out. Whatomato never retries those, since a retry could deliver a second copy.
- **Server downtime.** A server that was down sends what it missed once when it's back, however late that is. A recurring message sends one copy, not one for each missed run, and then moves on to its next time.

The Messages page warns you when WhatsApp isn't in a state to send.
