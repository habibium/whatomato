## Templates

A template is text you can drop into a message when you write one. Open **Templates** and choose **New template**, give it a name and write the text. A name can be up to 100 characters and the text up to 4096.

<Screenshot name="template-form" alt="The New template dialog with a name and a message that holds two variables" />

To use one:

- on the Templates page, choose **Use** on a template, which opens a new message with its text; or
- in the message form, pick it under **Apply template**, which replaces whatever the message says with the template's text.

<Screenshot name="template-use" alt="A new message opened from the Payment reminder template, with its text in the Message field and a preview of how it will read" />

The text is copied into the message at that moment. **Editing a template later doesn't change messages already made from it**, and deleting a template leaves their text alone. Each template shows how many messages use it.

<Screenshot name="templates-list" alt="The Templates page with four templates, each showing its text, how many messages use it and a Use button" />

## Variables

A variable is a name in double braces that Whatomato replaces as each message goes out. Type `{{` in a message or a template and pick one from the list.

<Screenshot name="message-variables" alt="The list of variables that opens after typing two opening braces in the Message field, each with an example for the chosen contact" />

```text
Hi {{first_name|there}}, a reminder that rent is due on {{date + 3d}}.
```

goes out to Amina on 27 November as

```text
Hi Amina, a reminder that rent is due on 30 November.
```

| Variable | Becomes | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{{first_name}}` | The person's first name, as saved on your phone | Amina |
| `{{name}}` | Their full name as saved on your phone, or the group's name | Amina Rahman |
| `{{day}}` | The day it goes out | Friday |
| `{{date}}` | The date it goes out | 27 November |
| `{{time}}` | The time it goes out | 9:05 PM |
| `{{month}}` | The month it goes out | November |
| `{{year}}` | The year it goes out | 2026 |
| `{{sunrise}}` | Sunrise on the day it goes out | 6:08 AM |
| `{{sunset}}` | Sunset on the day it goes out | 5:16 PM |

### When there's no value

A fallback goes after a bar. `{{first_name|there}}` says "there" when there's no first name, which is always the case for a group. Without a fallback, a variable with no value leaves nothing behind, so `Hi {{first_name}}!` would go out as `Hi !`. The form warns you when a variable would come out empty for the recipient you chose.

### Moving a time

Any of the time variables can move by minutes, hours or days: `{{sunset + 10m}}`, `{{time - 30m}}`, `{{date + 1d}}`. Use a plain hyphen for minus.

### Things to know

- Day and month names are in English, and times are in 12-hour form with AM or PM, whatever language your phone or the recipient's uses.
- Dates and times read in the message's own time zone.
- They describe the time the message was **due**. A message that leaves late, after a retry for instance, still says its planned time.
- Saving refuses a variable that doesn't exist, so a typo can't go out as raw braces.

[Message variables](/reference/variables/) has the full grammar and every rule.
